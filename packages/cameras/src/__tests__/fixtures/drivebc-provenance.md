# DriveBC inventory fixture

`ca-bc-drivebc.csv` contains 61 unmodified records from the Ministry-owned [CKAN CSV](https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/6b39a910-6c77-476f-ac96-7b4f18849b1c/resource/a9d52d85-8402-4ce7-b2ac-a2779837c48a/download/webcams.csv), fetched 2026-11-07. The selection retains every nonempty credit and groups 520, 32 and 914. CSV quoting and the UTF-8 BOM were normalized by Python's CSV writer. The complete capture contains 1,041 views; its SHA-256 is `cb6170129ba2efa247550acd32d8b098a009c621f5b10b33d8f63e6fe12fb6e0`. No pixels were acquired.

Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – British Columbia. [OGL-BC v2](https://pub.data.gov.bc.ca/license/OGL-vbc2.0.pdf) applies to this catalogue product. The [Ministry's webcam project](https://github.com/bcgov/drivebc-webcam-api) expressly releases images and data for commercial use. The [release announcement](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2016TRAN0045-000360) corroborates that product release. Credits are retained as published, including cooperation and imagery notices; they contain no contrary reservation in this capture. Actual future exceptions must be excluded before publication. CKAN's generic open/content classification flags are inconsistent with the express product grant.

Catalogue membership defines scope. Website-only partner views, archives and replay are excluded; a shared image host grants no rights. The CSV gives no image time, refresh interval or availability. Retrieval time and HTTP success never establish image age or camera operation. Elevation is omitted because its unit is unqualified.

The feed ships disabled and its snapshot qualification is pending. Operators must independently qualify complete acquisition and deletion semantics before overriding both controls. `minimumRecords` is a qualified lower bound, not proof of completeness; the shipped value prevents empty or major partial inventories, and must be reassessed for legitimate inventory changes. Syntax, transport success and matching row accounting alone cannot qualify a snapshot. Only a qualified complete inventory may withdraw missing features; withdrawal does not imply an outage or road closure. Failed acquisition and failed qualification retain the last good publication. Conservative daily metadata retrieval acquires no pixels; existing controlled image delivery must respect publisher revalidation directives.

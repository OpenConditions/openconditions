# Canadian MSC/CA surface observations

`ca-eccc-weather` reads GeoMet `swob-stations` and `swob-realtime`. Membership requires a station published with `data_provider=MSC` and `dataset_network=CA`, joined by its MSC identifier to `msc-observation-atmospheric-surface_weather-ca-1.1-ascii` readings with `data_pvdr-value=MSC`. These are publisher and network labels, not proof of hardware ownership. Other networks, marine data, partner datasets and unmatched observations are excluded. Station-level measurements have no fabricated sensor component. CRS84 longitude and latitude are retained; elevation is omitted from the two-dimensional geometry.

The [product overview](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/msc-data/obs_station/readme_obs_insitu_en/) expressly applies the [ECCC Data Services End-use Licence](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/licence/readme_en/) to GeoMet observations. The licence header is version 2.1.1, August 2026; section 9 still says 2.1. The catalogue uses `LicenseRef-ECCC-Data-Servers-End-use` and credits “Data Source: Environment and Climate Change Canada”. The product grant includes agreed contributor redistribution rights. It excludes unauthorized third-party rights, personal information and official marks. No such material is deliberately acquired. An additional `data_attrib_not-value` originator notice is excluded pending scope review; the exact ECCC credit is accepted and preserved by the catalogue attribution. This does not classify every partner as restricted.

## Measurement policy

The cached official SWOB-XML guide used for implementation is version 8.17, July 17, 2026, sections 4.5–4.7 and 6.5.3. Only numeric QA100 is accepted. QA−10 is suppressed, −1 missing, 0 error, 10 doubtful, 15 warning and 20 inconsistent. Missing QA, unknown codes, arrays, coerced strings and malformed values are not QA100. Values require finite numbers, exact published units and physical bounds. Null, blank, MSNG and coded missing values are omitted.

The supported measurements are temperature (`°C` to `Cel`), relative humidity (`%`), ten-minute mean wind speed (`km/h` divided by 3.6 to `m/s`), its direction (`°` to `deg`), and hourly precipitation (`mm`, sum). Intervals end at the source observation time. Any data flag present in the retrieved properties excludes the affected measurement, including derived (1), estimate (2), adjusted (3), incomplete (4) and trace (5). Trace is not converted to a precise zero. The configured projection retains the advertised wind and precipitation flag fields. Cached GeoMet queryables do not advertise temperature or humidity flag fields, and the captured sample has none; QA100 for those two channels cannot establish exclusion of qualifiers the provider does not expose. The parser rejects such flags if supplied, but this is not a claim of universal five-channel flag coverage. Dew point, gust, pressure, snow depth, road state, forecasts, hydrology and buoy measurements are outside this implementation.

Observation and processing times remain distinct. The observation timestamp must agree with `obs_date_tm`, must not be in the future and must be less than an hour old. Expiry is stored as observation time plus one hour, so API reads expire during outages and restarts without a poll. Provenance retains the source filename, processing time, correction indicator when supplied, and official source URI. Revisited records are ordered by correction level (`orig`, `CCA` … `CCZ`) then processing time. Conflicting equal ranks fail the candidate rather than depending on response order. Only one latest reading per station/property is emitted. Under the publication lock, durable per-channel authority compares measurement time, then correction level and processing time across polls. Explicit invalid values or QA suppression expire the prior public reading; their authority survives replay and restart. An absent channel alone carries no suppression authority.

## Acquisition and publication

Metadata is requested daily and current readings every ten minutes over a fixed-end 90-minute overlap. The overlap catches delayed arrivals and corrections within that window. It is not a change cursor, a historical mirror, a correction-latency guarantee or evidence that missing observations were deleted. Older corrections are outside the supported acquisition horizon. Missing readings retain their previous values until source expiry; they do not remove stations. A rights-changing feature generation is refused while retained readings carry the earlier rights and credit. When a complete inventory withdraws a feature, its retained readings expire in that same transaction. Only a validated complete station response can authorize feature withdrawal.

Both roles require one response whose integer `numberMatched`, `numberReturned`, actual feature count and unique feature identifiers agree, with no next link and fewer than 10,000 features. Requests use explicit limits of 1,000 stations and 2,000 observations. A capped or partial response fails closed; there is no unbounded page walk or automatic enlargement. A larger future network needs separately qualified bounded time slices before these limits can be raised. Empty station inventories cannot withdraw the network. Empty status responses retain metadata and let readings expire. The provider's observed `id`/multi-sort HTTP200-empty trap is avoided by sorting only on observation date.

Responses are limited to 8 MiB during acquisition and parsing, with the shared guarded-fetch deadline, cancellation and address checks. Failed attempts keep the publication, payload/index state and HTTP validators; another scheduled attempt is the retry, at the ten-minute cadence rather than an immediate request loop. Operators must back off or stop polling if service errors persist. Requests carry a meaningful User-Agent and no no-cache headers. These cadences and local request limits are engineering choices, not provider quotas or an SLA. The [usage policy](https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/usage-policy/readme_en/) requires coordination at 86,400 requests/day or higher. Systematic Datamart acquisition requires AMQPS and directory availability polling is prohibited. This implementation makes no Datamart requests.

The format opts into atomic canonical validation under the source publication lock. Invalid drafts reject the whole candidate before any publication/status commit; storage rejections also abort the transaction. Held-role state is restored on failure, and validators are accepted only after successful publication. The public API, federation and archive use the existing record-rights pipeline; only qualified MSC/CA records reach it. Fixture tests establish deterministic behavior. The separate `ECCC_LIVE=1` integration run exercises current anonymous GeoMet acquisition through PostGIS and public API; it is a dated qualification, not production or long-term completeness proof.

## Findings that shaped the decoders

- The OCPDB association's `id` equals the `id` its tariff has on the 2.2 tariffs
  endpoint; its `tariff_id` (131404 for 138586) resolves nowhere on that
  endpoint, and the connector `tariff_ids` hold hashes (`original_id`s) that
  match no association. OMX's older fixtures had `id` equal to `tariff_id`.
- NDW and OCPDB tariffs carry nulls everywhere (dropped by the decoder) and `0`
  for an unset `max_duration`.
- Lithuania sends strings for prices and VAT, `voltage`/`amperage` instead of
  `max_*`, times with seconds, and times without a zone (`last_updated` in two
  naive forms). Location ids are numbers.
- Digitraffic EVSEs have no `uid` and its connectors no id; the statuses
  endpoint carries the `lastUpdatedAt` of the operator and the `modifiedAt` of
  Digitraffic, which can be later than the former.

# Iowa AVL evidence

`iowa-dot-avl.json.txt` is an unchanged copy of the repository's historical September 19, 2026 infrastructure fixture. Its original retrieval URL, headers and retrieval instant were not recorded with those bytes; the ping is not a retrieval time. It is historical wire-shape evidence and does not qualify current LABEL continuity. All mutations in tests are synthetic, including moved pings and rekeyed OBJECTIDs.

`iowa-current.json.txt` is an unedited bounded public response; its URL, retrieval instant, headers, byte length and SHA-256 are in `iowa-current.provenance.json`. This capture uses only the seven selected attributes and WGS84 geometry, one page of at most 500 rows. Empty success confirms only the selected population at retrieval.

`iowa-item.json.txt` is the exact item metadata downloaded during this implementation from https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/20a0c10c06a54240b5f2893e0187e22c?f=json . The item supplies the CC BY 4.0 licence and Iowa Department of Transportation - Office of Maintenance credit. The source attribution retains the item, licence and disclaimer links, normalization notice and no-endorsement statement. Product-specific machine access was accepted in the final November 7 review; the enabled catalogue entry uses the accepted scoped interpretation without a rights override.

Blade states, garage/cost-centre fields, weather, run totals and personal enrichment are excluded from acquisition and production output. No numeric upstream request quota is claimed. Acquisition uses the shared scheduler at 120 seconds, at most ten pages of 500 rows, a 10 MiB cycle cap and a 60-second cycle deadline. Paged replies are fetched in sequence without conditional validators because a validator for one page cannot validate a whole moving selected set. Source ping expiry remains independent of successful acquisition.

`iowa-live-1.json.txt` and `iowa-live-2.json.txt` preserve complete, unedited November 9 cohorts. `iowa-live.provenance.json` records metadata, counts, IDs, query URLs, UTC retrieval times, headers and SHA-256. Each count and ID response reconciles to all 39 rows on its single terminal page. The cohorts were 144 seconds apart and byte-identical; they demonstrate unique LABEL continuity within this interval, not sustained updates or a permanent identifier guarantee. Every captured material code is NONE. Other documented material codes and adversarial cases remain explicitly synthetic tests. No third cohort was polled.

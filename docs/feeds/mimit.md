# MIMIT daily fuel bulletin

`it-mimit-fuel` joins the ministry's [active registry and daily prices](https://www.mimit.gov.it/it/open-data/elenco-dataset/carburanti-prezzi-praticati-e-anagrafica-degli-impianti) by the exact textual `idImpianto`. The two required endpoints run at a daily cadence through ordinary bounded acquisition, caching and backoff. A failed role cannot authorize a replacement from held bytes. The parser does not access the real-time consumer portal or an alternate relay.

The catalogue entry is disabled pending a complete paired raw CSV capture from the acquisition network. On 7 November 2026, each canonical CSV request timed out after 20 seconds without headers or payload bytes. The committed CSV fixtures are invented schema examples, not ministry captures. Their headers, pipe delimiter and extraction preamble follow the [ministry metadata](https://www.mimit.gov.it/images/stories/documenti/Metadati_prezzi_carburanti_20260128.pdf) and [AgID's government ETL](https://github.com/AgID/cruscotto-italia/blob/1b8fa8544a5ef58295838c82bfa8daa13965fef1/etl/sources/carburanti.py). AgID is corroboration only; its use of real-time portal prices does not qualify the daily price file.

On 8 November 2026, a new coordinated pair failed during connection after approximately 12 seconds per GET, with no HTTP response, headers or body bytes. These failures establish a reachability blocker from this qualification host, not a denial, rate limit or dataset discontinuation. The activation receipt retains curl diagnostics and the request budget.

Before activation, retain both complete transfers and their retrieval instants, URLs, byte lengths, SHA-256 digests and HTTP metadata. Confirm actual UTF-8 decoding, exact preamble/header/row widths and record terminators against the parser. Check that both extraction dates match, that counts and joins are plausible, and that the transport delivered complete bodies. A row-boundary truncation cannot be proved absent from CSV syntax, a final newline, Content-Length, plausible counts or repeated short responses. These checks never qualify withdrawal authority. A provider census or withdrawal contract is not required to operate a conservative partial source: leave both role qualifications absent, retain omitted records and enforce the original cutoff expiry. Current matched raw transfers and wire qualification are still required to enable that useful partial scope. Unexpected quoting or columns fail closed for review. Do not silently repair a publication or substitute another source.

The price phenomenon time is 08:00 on the extraction date, interpreted as Europe/Rome civil time. The daily release can describe the preceding day; the parser does not subtract another day from the dated header. Operator `dtComu` becomes `provenance.sourceUpdatedAt`, with the original communication text and extraction date in allow-listed extras. `freshness.fetchedAt` remains the acquisition time. No publication clock time is invented. Winter and summer fixtures test the Rome conversion. Communication fields must have valid calendar and clock values and round-trip exactly through Europe/Rome. Nonexistent spring times and ambiguous autumn times reject the publication; there is no source-qualified fold policy, so neither candidate instant is asserted. The shared scheduling converter keeps its existing behavior for other callers. The ministry documentation does not explicitly supply an offset: confirm the timezone assumption and transition behavior before activation.

Coordinates are voluntarily supplied decimal degrees and are not certified survey positions. The implementation uses `[longitude, latitude]` in the model's geographic geometry and accepts placeable points within a broad Italy envelope. The retrieved documentation does not specify an EPSG datum. Qualify this interpretation against the provider before activation. Rows with missing or unusable positions are counted as rejected; their presence makes feature and reading withdrawal incomplete, preserving last-good records while usable rows can still update.

The local source-age policy accepts cutoffs from the past 48 hours and rejects future cutoffs. This acquisition-age limit accommodates the delayed daily publication; it is not a provider validity guarantee or an extension of public price freshness. A delayed export can contribute station inventory and retained readings while its prices are already expired in public output. Public price gaps are expected when publication lag exceeds 24 hours. Prices expire exactly 24 hours after the cutoff, preserving the global `fuel.price` freshness window regardless of polling success, through persisted `freshness.expiresAt`, API filtering and fusion. A transaction under the source lock retains the most recent accepted cutoff in `source_status.publication_at`, including after a complete empty publication. Older exports cannot replace it. Corrected exports at the same cutoff remain processable. Station inventory has no invented price expiry. Withdrawal authority is opt-in and independent for each role. In the existing catalogue `snapshot` declaration (`completeness: "complete"`), `roleEvidence.registry` must name retained, reviewed provider evidence establishing the registry’s completeness/continuity contract before missing stations may withdraw. Independently, `roleEvidence.prices` must name evidence for the price export before omitted price series may end. Neither reference is configured in this disabled feed. A reference is an operator assertion backed by evidence, not an automatic verification mechanism: retain the provider contract, capture/provenance and acceptance review, including legitimate subset/empty semantics, and remove the reference when that contract no longer holds. Enabling the feed alone grants neither authority. One qualified role never qualifies the other. Usable unqualified subsets and empty publications retain omitted records while advancing the durable publication watermark; retained prices still expire at their original cutoff-based limit. Existing shrink tripwires remain additional safeguards for explicitly qualified withdrawals. Errors, mismatched generations and unplaceable rows do not authorize withdrawal. Withdrawal says only that a station is absent from the publication, not physically closed.

Benzina maps to generic `petrol`, with no inferred octane or ethanol blend. Gasolio, GPL and Metano map to diesel, LPG and CNG respectively. Prices retain three decimals in EUR, per litre except CNG per kilogram. Self-service and attended prices are independent products. Unmapped marketing grades are counted as unknown values and excluded. No stock, opening status or availability is inferred; products are not declared complete.

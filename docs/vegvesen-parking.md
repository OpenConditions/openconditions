# Norway parking inventory qualification

`no-vegvesen-parking` reads static structured inventory from Statens vegvesen's [Parkeringsregisteret](https://dataut.vegvesen.no/dataset/parkeringsregisteret). Its scope is registered conditional parking. It does not establish comprehensive coverage of all parking or universal public access. The [registration guide](https://www.vegvesen.no/fag/trafikk/parkering/for-parkeringstilbydere/utfylling-av-felter) explains area splitting and future activation. The [dedicated read API](https://parkreg-open.atlas.vegvesen.no/ws/docs/openapi) documents `alle`, `kart` and optional organization filtering, with no pagination contract. This feed uses two national collection requests, with no per-ID fan-out, contacts join or OSM enrichment.

The feed is enabled after a national qualification on 2026-11-09. The full export returned 22,958 unique records in 172,772,490 bytes and 31.332 seconds; the map returned the same IDs in 7,971,153 bytes and 5.674 seconds. Every coordinate agreed across projections. The inventory contained 10,877 active records and 12,081 deactivated records, with 103,427 historical versions excluded. All active points were within a broad Norway sanity envelope; known official controls 32771 and 98316 retained their geographic points, totals and leading-zero postcodes. The formal datum remains unstated. These are response counts within the register's scope, not an exhaustive census of parking.

The initial qualification used incremental transient decoding under predeclared 512 MiB / 180-second full-export and 16 MiB / 45-second map budgets, with a 1 GiB process-memory ceiling. Peak RSS was 122,388,480 bytes for that acquisition and sanitization process. Only allowlisted current structured fields, response hashes, counts and timing evidence were persisted. Production acquisition uses a measured-headroom 256 MiB / 120-second full-export budget and the same map budget. `parkreg.acquisitionSha256` binds the reviewed endpoints, authorization, pacing and raw retention; changing any of those requires renewed qualification. The parser independently rejects unqualified or substituted configurations. No numerical quota or refresh SLA is documented in the inspected source documentation; cadence and budgets are OpenConditions engineering choices. Respect Retry-After on failures and avoid repeated national downloads during routine development.

An eligible poll requires exactly one full collection and one map inventory with identical unique numeric IDs. An ID mismatch, duplicate ID, malformed collection, invalid active row, network error or budget failure rejects the replacement and preserves the last publication. The parser accepts only the exact ordinary national `main=alle` and `inventory=kart` URLs, without additional query parameters, alternate endpoints or acquisition transforms, and requires a matching acquisition hash. Organization-filtered and historical configurations cannot authorize this national source. Equal IDs establish cross-projection consistency, but cannot detect correlated truncation or an erroneous matched empty array from both national endpoints; qualification and shrink safeguards remain necessary. A qualified empty answer or an answer containing only inactive/future/ended records explicitly permits an empty feature snapshot. A poll with an acquisition warning cannot authorize replacement, including when a scheduler has a held response available. Existing shrink guards still apply. This includes legitimate active-to-deactivated withdrawals. No-payload and unqualified empty responses have no such authority.

Only records with explicit null `deaktivert`, a current valid version and an active date interval publish a feature. Historical versions never create additional sites. Identity is the numeric area ID across revisions. Paid plus fee-free spaces form total capacity; disabled and charging counts are overlapping subsets and are never added to that total. Missing or null counts remain unknown: the total is omitted unless both paid and fee-free counts are known, and missing subset counts produce no component. The national record 2206 had an unknown fee-free count; it remains a valid site with no invented total. Malformed, negative or unsafe counts still reject the snapshot. Unknown or unspecified area types also reject it because the model requires a concrete site type; unknown park-and-ride tokens leave that usage unspecified. Garages are off-street without an inferred underground or multistorey layout. Coordinates are publisher geographic-degree points; the API does not formally identify a datum. No boundary is inferred.

Source version and modification time describe inventory revision. Retrieval is separately recorded as `fetchedAt`; no occupancy observation freshness is invented. The source emits no occupancy observations, prices, offers, opening hours, charger connectors or access guarantees. `rawRetention: none` prevents archiving full responses or holding role payloads between polls, including their assessment prose and media references; normalized output retains only the structured fields the parser selects. No PDF or image is fetched. The conservative daily cadence and two-day source freshness window are implementation choices, not publisher service guarantees.

The [product metadata](https://dataut.vegvesen.no/api/3/action/package_show?id=parkeringsregisteret) assigns NLOD, and the [national catalogue](https://data.norge.no/en/datasets/af5a2e69-7be0-30bd-99b1-734697cf72e5/parkeringsregisteret) identifies [NLOD 2.0](https://data.norge.no/nlod/no/2.0). Source credit, versioned licence link and the OpenConditions modification notice remain in catalogue attribution and public output. Upstream public-egress rights remain default-deny; this addition grants no rights to overlapping OSM records.

With `rawRetention: none`, every due poll obtains complete bodies for all roles without conditional validators and re-evaluates activation and end dates. An unexpected 304 is retried once unconditionally; a repeated 304 fails and preserves last-good publication. Conditional body caches, per-item kept responses and role payload/URL/item/answer caches are neither reused nor retained in this mode. Ordinary caching and monitoring defaults are unchanged.

# JMA ingestion

The `jma-xml` worker uses durable acquisition and native report reconciliation. It does not parse JMAXML as CAP. Both catalogue feeds are enabled for the supported native observed-report scope. The earthquake worker has a PostGIS/API regression; the warning worker requires an explicit incomplete-coverage contract because an authoritative bootstrap has not been qualified. Rolling recent and long indexes provide incomplete coverage, including after recovery. A successful poll records `partial`, never a complete active-warning census.

Every process sharing a provider-visible egress IP must use the same database and the same explicit `OPENCONDITIONS_JMA_EGRESS_ID`. Both JMA feeds share this key. Separate databases or inconsistent keys do not enforce a shared quota. The daily allowance is 8,000,000,000 bytes per UTC day, below JMA's 10 GB threshold. Each request reserves its maximum body allowance before transport. A completed read settles to observed body bytes. Transport or guard failures can conceal bytes already consumed, so their reservations remain charged for that day, including across restarts. Uncertainty can exhaust the day's allowance; it is never blindly refunded. Eight days of counters are retained. One transient transport/5xx retry uses another reservation and a short abortable delay within the poll deadline.

Payload URLs are restricted to the official HTTPS data path with redirects refused. Recent indexes have a one-minute cadence and long indexes an hourly cadence. Polls are serial, bounded to 1,000 selected messages per channel and 8 MB per response. The warning worker reads two channels, for at most 2,000 selected messages in total, sharing one two-minute deadline and the same daily egress byte budget. Families are filtered before payload acquisition. Immutable bodies persist across restarts. Files outside a 45-day download horizon are refused; bodies older than 45 days are removed during reservation. The cache refuses new entries at 25,000 rows. These ceilings fail the poll and preserve the last publication; they do not assert complete recovery.

Earthquake publication retains thirty days by the source Control revision clock. Older earthquake units and tombstones are removed together, and incoming reports behind the same advancing floor are ignored. The long index cannot resurrect a removed unit behind that floor. An event folds complementary fields from uncancelled families in Control-clock order into one situation. A newer empty or nil OriginTime clears an older origin, while a genuinely omitted OriginTime preserves complementary information. Unknown origin leaves validity.start absent; no report-clock occurrence is substituted. Each contributing report retains its URL, clock and native XML; cancelling a family removes only that family’s contribution. Warning state retains omitted areas and explicit release tombstones; only a supplied source `ValidDateTime` can expire a warning. Storage shares each native report across area references, with a maximum 20,000 areas, 4,096 product units and 32 MB serialized lifecycle state. Exceeding these limits rolls back publication and cursor updates.

Rights policy reviewed on 2026-11-09: the worker allows only the reviewed JMA offices 気象庁, 気象庁本庁, 札幌管区気象台 横浜地方気象台 and 新潟地方気象台. This identity subset uses [JMA’s CC BY 4.0 alternative](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/en/copyright.html), preserving “Japan Meteorological Agency; normalized by OpenConditions (CC BY 4.0)” and the source/licence links. OpenConditions is responsible for normalization under the [XML reuse notice](https://xml.kishou.go.jp/considerationforxml.pdf). Native wording, authority, offices and credits remain in the original XML. No GIS geometry, logos or independent forecasts are included.

Numeric and predefined XML character references are decoded once for inspection and selected fields, while the original XML bytes are retained. DTDs, custom entities, comments, CDATA and processing instructions other than the initial XML declaration are refused. Known namespace prefixes must keep their supported bindings, including in nested scopes; unsupported aliases and namespaced attributes are refused. This is a supported-subset contract, not full XSD validation.

The gate inspects both decoded publishing/editorial offices and every retained native element and attribute, including all OfficeInfo/Office/Name entries. The closed native-element inventory excludes unreviewed extensions, including new credit fields. Embedded rights/source/author attributes and explicit third-party credit or restriction markers also withhold the affected warning product/office unit. Earthquake polls retain whole-poll rights refusal. This conservative semantic policy is not an XSD validator or a claim that arbitrary future prose is rights-cleared. New offices, elements, upstream credits and restriction forms require evidence and an explicit policy review before admission; merely naming JMA in PublishingOffice never clears a partner.

The cached R06 manual section 3-5 identifies OfficeInfo as participating-agency information; its transitional guidance separately permits JMA-only PublishingOffice. All seven retained official VPWW56 timeline samples name 神奈川県県土整備局河川下水道部砂防課. No separate permission evidence was found or invented for that authority, so those complete reports are withheld, including releases. Their bytes and credit remain unchanged in the attributed test samples. A separately named synthetic JMA-only VPWW56 sample exercises positive family publication; it is derived from the JMA-only rain sample by changing the family title and level-2 wording/code, and is not provider evidence of an actual landslide publication.

Warning rights exclusions freeze the affected family/event/editorial-office unit, including all its areas, at its last-good content and expiry. The version-2 lifecycle JSON stores only the withheld Control revision clock and native-byte SHA-256, not the restricted report. All withheld floors in a batch are established before admitting any reports: older or equal-clock admissible replays cannot update that unit, in either input order or after restart. A strictly newer independently qualified revision can resume it. Other offices and products remain independent. The admitted canonical publication, floors, last-success cursor and initialization receipt commit in one transaction. Immutable private download bodies and HTTP validators remain separately durable transport state; they never authorize lifecycle publication. Malformed XML and unsupported native semantics still refuse the whole poll, and admissible warning message cancellations remain unsupported. Index absence never withdraws retained units.

## Incomplete warning initialization and recovery

The default runtime gate refuses warnings. Set `OPENCONDITIONS_JMA_WARNING_COVERAGE=incomplete`, configure the shared egress identity, and explicitly enable the alerts source in that environment’s catalogue. This accepts only observed-report coverage and never asserts that unobserved areas are clear. The catalogue is enabled; operators must configure the shared egress identity and explicitly accept incomplete warning coverage.

The first successful worker poll reads both recent and long indexes for weather and volcano families, decodes selected reports and commits their materialized per-area state with canonical publication. The same transaction stores an initialization receipt containing the source ID, initialization time, SHA-256 payload identities, `coverage=incomplete` and `method=rolling-index-observed-reports`. An empty initial index can produce an empty observed state; the receipt still explicitly declares incomplete coverage. Failed acquisition, unidentifiable/unsupported native input or publication cannot commit that receipt. Identified rights-held warning units can coexist with admitted units in the incomplete receipt. Restart loads the receipt, retained areas, releases and product cursors from the database. After a gap, long-index recovery adds observed reports without treating absent reports as withdrawals. Qualification must inspect `/sources`, native `/situations` and individual records alongside the persisted receipt; a successful request alone does not qualify coverage.

There is no importer for an authoritative active-warning census and no switch that promotes this receipt to complete coverage. Such an importer would require a separately reviewed authority, snapshot identity, scope, acquisition and restart evidence. The incomplete mode provides a testable ingestion/recovery path without making that claim.

A cache-only poll does not advance network freshness. Successful network reads and conditional validation are tracked separately from immutable payload acquisition. Situation `freshness.fetchedAt` retains the original payload acquisition time across restarts. Poll receipts include selected payload SHA-256 identities even when those bodies came from the durable cache. These hashes identify bytes; they do not imply that a raw archive object exists.

Raw archive capture is unsupported in this dedicated path. When `RunDeps.raw` is configured the JMA worker refuses before acquisition or publication, pending rights qualification and an archive implementation. The private download cache is an acquisition implementation detail, not a public raw archive. Uncleared third-party material is deliberately excluded under the admission policy above. This exclusion does not require partner contact or blanket clearance.

## Supported native semantic subset

The decoder performs semantic validation against the pinned operational header matrix and code tables, rather than full XSD validation. The supported headers are VXSE51/52/53 1.0_0 and 1.0_1, VPWW55/56/57 1.5_0 and VFVO50 1.0_0. Unknown families, versions, Control status, InfoType, namespaces and warning sections fail closed. Weather code/wording pairs follow R06 table 3. VFVO50 accepts level-based target-volcano codes 11–15, target-municipality codes 01–05 and disaster-response codes 41–49 from table 81; other volcanic systems require additional semantic review. Message cancellation for warnings is unsupported and rolls back the poll; it is never interpreted as a warning release. Downgrades and explicit releases remain distinct from message cancellation.

Earthquake coordinates use the reviewed world-datum degree representation. Explicit alternative datum/unit metadata and ambiguous multiple coordinates are refused. The degree-minute helper is tested separately; volcanic coordinates remain native XML and do not become warning geometry. Qualitative depth descriptions never become exact depths. Magnitude NaN remains unknown. Native shaking observations retain prefecture, area, municipality and station identities in separate code systems, with at most 10,000 observations per report. Whitespace around intensity tokens is normalized while the original XML remains intact. Unknown tokens fail closed and are never converted to MMI or CDI.

## Activation evidence (2026-11-09)

Current bounded operational captures are retained under the hazards fixtures with a byte manifest. VXSE51/53 operational version 1.0_1 now decodes alongside the previously supported versions. Niigata is a reviewed JMA office (https://www.data.jma.go.jp/niigata/); the whole-report embedded-authority gate remains in force. Current Tokyo VPWW56 and retained Kanagawa partner reports remain withheld. Kanagawa’s current site policy and landslide-system terms do not establish the needed redistribution permission (https://www.pref.kanagawa.jp/master/sitepolicy.html and https://www.pref.kanagawa.jp/docs/jy2/dosyaho/terms.html). No partner permission is inferred from JMA hosting. Warning admission is independent by family, event and editorial office. Uncleared reports retain a durable withheld revision floor while unrelated admissible units can publish. This F2 recovery correction does not establish full captured recovery or interruptible decoder isolation.

The stateless feeds checker reports an explicit warning without fetching JMA files: it cannot coordinate the worker’s persistent cache, shared quota or warning lifecycle. The acquisition/worker/PostGIS/API receipt is separate. Operational-byte replay uses an explicitly bounded selection index, not the complete provider index. JMA uses its dedicated in-process native decoder rather than the generic parser child. Full-source recovery and parser-child isolation are not established by that replay.
